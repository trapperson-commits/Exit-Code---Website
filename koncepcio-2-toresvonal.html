We’re Exit Code, a nu metal band from Berlin. Ádám on guitar. Zsolt on drums. Heavy music built around one belief: however hard life gets, there is always a way forward.

Our name is an invitation to step beyond limiting systems and patterns. To find yourself, reconnect with nature and build something better together.

Creativity. Mutual respect. Care for people, animals and the Earth. That’s what drives us.
