We are Exit Code. Two founders. One shared belief: however hard life gets, there is always a way forward.

From Berlin, Ádám and Zsolt turn that belief into nu metal. Our name is an invitation to step beyond limiting systems and patterns, find yourself and reconnect with what matters.

People. Nature. Creativity. Respect. Through heavy music, we want to bring people together and build a more liveable world.
